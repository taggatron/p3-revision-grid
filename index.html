GCSE Gateway Physics P3 Formula Sheet

Key equations, SI units, and relationships assessed in Electricity & Magnetism.

Equation Word Formula Standard SI Units
Q = I × t Charge = Current × Time Charge in Coulombs (C), Current in Amperes (A), Time in seconds (s)
E = Q × V Energy = Charge × Potential Difference Energy in Joules (J), Charge in Coulombs (C), Voltage in Volts (V)
V = I × R Potential Difference = Current × Resistance Voltage in Volts (V), Current in Amperes (A), Resistance in Ohms (Ω)
P = V × I Power = Potential Difference × Current Power in Watts (W), Voltage in Volts (V), Current in Amperes (A)
P = I² × R Power = Current² × Resistance Power in Watts (W), Current in Amperes (A), Resistance in Ohms (Ω)
E = P × t Energy = Power × Time Joules (J) = Watts (W) × seconds (s)
OR kWh = kW × hours
F = B × I × l Force = Flux Density × Current × Length Force in Newtons (N), B in Tesla (T), Current in Amps (A), Length in metres (m)

⚡ Essential Unit Conversions & Multipliers

  • 1 mA = 0.001 A (÷ 1000)
  • 1 kW = 1000 W (× 1000)
  • 1 hour = 3600 seconds
  • 1 kWh = 3,600,000 J (3.6 MJ)
  • 1 kΩ = 1000 Ω
  • 1 µA = 0.000001 A (10⁻⁶ A)
